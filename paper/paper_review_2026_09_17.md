Paper review, 17 September 2026

**Scope and changes.** This review compares the research diary with the current source, saved training configurations and histories, and result CSVs at revision `02073d0`. The paper now has an updated title and abstract, a dated reproducibility and interpretation section, corrected introductory physics, and retrospective notices on historical results. The experimental chronology and archived numerical results are preserved. Training code and saved experiments were not changed. The overview for advisors is in `advisor_overview_2026_09_17.md`; `review_evidence.py` reproduces the split and paired-temperature audit in `review_evidence_2026_09_17.json`.

**Overall assessment.** The diary records substantial diagnostic work and explains why successive experiments were attempted. Its principal weakness was that old conclusions, planned work, and completed results could all read as current. The abstract stopped before the molecular-exclusion ZBL and fine-tuning results. Several statements also gave stronger physical or statistical interpretations than their evidence supports. The dated review now provides a current interpretation while retaining the earlier reasoning for future reference.

**Data partitioning is the most consequential finding.** Training and the matrix runner's static evaluation use matching seeds. Rollout evaluation uses seed 42 independently of checkpoint training seed. For the documented 1,593-entry ordering, the six rollout starts map to dataset indices 234, 1446, 23, 1133, 514, and 1083. All are training examples for seeds 0 and 1. This is a confirmed mismatch under the source-defined ordering, and it invalidates the description of those starts as held out from those checkpoints. It does not erase the measured heating or the within-start comparisons. Results should be presented as exploratory dynamics diagnostics.

**Fine-tuning also needs a persistent split.** Its default seed-42 training pool contains 129 original seed-1 test configurations. Membership in the pool establishes possible supervised exposure, not the number actually drawn. The default observable reference sample includes 16 configurations from the seed-42 test set and 23 from the original seed-1 test set. Instability floors explicitly use all 160 seed-42 test configurations when the requested reference count is 200. Thus the nominal test partition influences fine-tuning. Full historical command lines are missing, so seed overrides and static before/after evaluation indices still need verification. Repeated consultation of the same test results during method development further makes them exploratory rather than a final untouched assessment.

**The physical interpretation needs correction.** A liquid-water molecule receives forces and torques from its environment. Penalizing its instantaneous net force and torque toward zero is an empirical restriction that may suppress legitimate motion. The paper now distinguishes this regularizer from a conservation-law residual. For an isolated energy-based model, the relevant conservation identities follow from scalar-energy translation and rotation invariance. The old attribution to equivariance alone was incomplete. The metric also combines squared force and torque with implicit unit-dependent scaling. General momentum balance is described in [TU Delft's mechanics text](https://interactivetextbooks.tudelft.nl/nb1140/content/momentum.html).

**Metric definitions need care.** The evaluator's maximum “per-molecule” value is actually a maximum over configuration-averaged molecular penalties. Its torque uses raw centered coordinates without explicit periodic molecular unwrapping, which calls for a boundary-representation test. The configured energy and force coefficients multiply differently scaled MSEs and are not shares of gradient contribution. WaterBox conversion to eV and Å is explicit. Aspirin's eV labels require verification: the project MD17 paths have no conversion, and the [upstream MD17 loader](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torchmd/torchmd-net/main/torchmdnet/datasets/md17.py) copies raw labels. The remote installed version and processed cache determine the actual units.

**The fine-tuning null result has a limited scope.** The local-run paired temperature difference is +3.57 K; an approximate paired t interval is −42.17 to +49.30 K. This conditional, five-pair result establishes no resolved improvement and leaves substantial uncertainty. It cannot establish equivalence or method-wide failure. The reported static energy regression requires matching evaluation indices before attributing its size to fine-tuning. The global/local comparison also changed the snapshot cap and has no replicated fine-tuning seeds, so it does not isolate a causal observable effect.

**Published-method fidelity and sampling remain open.** [StABlE's method description](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.13984v3) uses instability-triggered phases and repeated learning from near-unstable starts. The project uses fixed sampling windows, continuing replicas, immediate rewinds on failures, accepted stable snapshots, and one update per phase. It is an adaptation whose sampling behavior needs validation. Fresh samples do not by themselves establish equilibrium or independence. Roughly 512 molecular entries from eight snapshots are correlated, and their effective sample size is unknown. Reference ensemble matching, stable-snapshot selection, the local-energy approximation, and the balance between observable and supervised gradients warrant inspection before attributing the null result to insufficient learning rate or to an uninformative observable.

**Statistical claims should be narrowed.** Molecule-pair tests cluster many observations inside each configuration, iteration-wise tests involve serial dependence, and selection of an improving replica after examining several replicas creates a multiple-comparison concern. Configuration-level or hierarchical uncertainty estimates are needed. Five velocity replicates of one model do not supply five independent trained models. Six-config force-error/heating correlations support an exploratory association. Requiring unanimous signs is not a general significance test, and mixed signs cannot prove an absent effect. These qualifications are now recorded beside the historical analyses.

**Reproducibility details added.** The paper now consolidates split counts and algorithms, model architecture, units, batch/accumulation settings, weights and annealing, scheduler parameters, checkpoint eligibility, ZBL settings, the latest reported fine-tuning recipe, rollout seeds and timestep, tail-temperature definition, and drift normalization. The saved best-score matches have history labels 68/68 for seed 0 and 63/66 for seed 1, rather than identical labels across all runs. Exact selected epochs need remote checkpoint metadata because callback timing may affect history labels. The `final_epoch` CSV column is the last history row. The [upstream optimizer implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torchmd/torchmd-net/main/torchmdnet/module.py) uses AdamW and a weighted-validation scheduler; matching the installed version remains necessary, particularly because the project's anneal-invariant checkpoint fix does not itself change the scheduler monitor.

**Evidence still needed.** Archive the remote software versions, GPU and precision settings, dataset hashes and exact indices, commands, checkpoint hashes and metadata, and complete fine-tuning settings and RNG states. Fine-tuning histories currently lack a complete run manifest, and locally there are no checkpoint binaries. The MD17 loaded count, unit convention, dataset state-point composition, trajectory correlations, and numerical literature comparisons remain incompletely verified. The literature check here focused on conservation, upstream loader/optimizer behavior, and StABlE; it was not an exhaustive audit of every citation.

**Recommended experimental priority.** First repair and preserve evaluation boundaries and determine which published training assumptions the adaptation satisfies. Then compare continued supervised training and observable-based fine-tuning at matched optimization budget, using identical untouched starts, explicit seed roles, and multiple base checkpoints. Report force/energy accuracy alongside heating, structural behavior, and time to a predefined instability. The existing 1 ps diagnostics do not establish long-time utility. These steps can distinguish implementation or sampling problems from a scientific negative result.

**Verification limits.** The split audit and paired calculation were rerun locally. LaTeX structure, references, citation keys, and the diff were checked. This machine has no LaTeX compiler, so PDF compilation and visual layout were not verified. No GPU experiments or checkpoint evaluations were rerun.
